Resilience

When the grid fails, your rack stays on.

Power is the first thing auditors ask about. Here is what happens, step by step, when the street loses power.

  • N+1UPS redundancy behind every rack
  • 0 msBreak in power when the grid drops
  • 48 hGenerator fuel on site at full load
  • 24/7Staff on site to respond
  1. 0 secondsUtility power dropsThe N+1 UPS carries every rack at once. Your servers never see the gap.
  2. About 10 secondsGenerators startDiesel generators start on their own and come up to speed while the UPS holds the load.
  3. Under a minuteGenerators take the loadAutomatic transfer switches move the building to generator power and the UPS batteries recharge.
  4. Grid restoredBack to utility powerThe load moves back with no break, and staff on site log the event and send you a report.
