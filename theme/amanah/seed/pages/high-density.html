Workloads

Three workloads, one dense hall.

Training, inference and HPC push a rack in different ways. Here is what each one asks of the building, and how TOR2 answers.

01 · Training

Weeks at full power.

Training holds every GPU near its peak for days on end, across nodes that need to sit close together.

How TOR2 answers

  • 25 kW per rack keeps nodes in neighbouring cabinets
  • N+1 UPS and generator behind every circuit
  • Unmetered 100 Gbps for dataset pulls

02 · Inference

Always on, close to users.

Serving models is steady load that follows your users. Latency and uptime matter more than peak draw.

How TOR2 answers

  • 400+ networks in the building
  • TorIX at 100 Gbps
  • Cloud Connect to AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and Cloudflare

03 · HPC and rendering

Bursts on a deadline.

Simulation and render queues spike to full power for a batch, then fall back. Someone may need to swap a drive at 2 a.m.

How TOR2 answers

  • Three-phase headroom for the peaks
  • Staff on site 24/7
  • Remote hands from $100 CAD an hour

GPU questions

What engineers ask first.

Something else on your list? Bring it to the sizing call, or ring the team on +1 416 603 9825.

Do you support liquid cooling?

TOR2 cabinets run up to 25 kW, with the building cooled through Enwave's deep lake water system. Rear-door heat exchangers and direct-to-chip loops are scoped case by case, so bring your hardware spec to the sizing call.

How is power billed?

Your written quote prices power per cabinet on 208 V three-phase circuits, sized to the draw you give us. You commit to a kW figure per rack and pay a flat monthly rate for it.

Can we ship our servers to you?

Yes. Ship to 151 Front Street West and we receive, log and hold your hardware until the rack is ready. Rack it yourself with unescorted access, or have us build and rack it for you.

What can remote hands do at 2 a.m.?

Staff are on site 24/7, and remote hands start at $100 CAD an hour: reseats, drive and DIMM swaps, recabling, console checks and photos of what we see.

Is the bandwidth really unmetered?

Yes. 100 Gbps ports are unmetered, so dataset pulls and inference traffic carry no overage bill. Port count per cabinet is confirmed in your quote. Carriers and TorIX are a cross connect away, with no cap.
