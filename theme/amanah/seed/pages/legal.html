Policy 01

Acceptable use policy

Everyone in the building shares the same power, cooling and network. This policy sets out what you can and cannot do with the space and connectivity you rent, so one customer's traffic never puts another's at risk.

What it covers

  • Activity and content that are not allowed on our network.
  • Spam, abuse and attacks sent from your addresses.
  • How we look into reports and how we work with you to fix them.
  • When service can be limited or suspended.

Policy 02

Service level agreement

The service level agreement describes what we commit to for power, cooling, network and support at 151 Front Street West, how we measure it, and what you receive if we fall short.

What it covers

  • The services each commitment applies to.
  • What counts as an outage, and what does not, such as planned maintenance.
  • How to report an incident and how we keep you updated.
  • How service credits are worked out and claimed.

Policy 03

Privacy policy

The privacy policy explains what personal information we collect from customers and visitors, why we collect it, where it is kept and the choices you have. We handle it under PIPEDA, and PHIPA where health information is involved.

What it covers

  • The information we collect, from account details to building access records.
  • Why we use it, and who we may share it with.
  • How long we keep it and how it is protected.
  • How to see, correct or ask us to delete your information.
